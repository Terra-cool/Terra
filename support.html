Support

How can we help?

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app—we read every message.

Email us

The fastest way to reach the Terra team.

support@terra.photos

Forgot your password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot password and we'll email you a reset link.

See a post that breaks the rules?

Tap Report on the post or comment. Photos with people in them aren't allowed, and our team reviews every report.

Think we removed your post by mistake?

Open Appeals in the app to appeal a moderation decision and track its status.

Want to delete your account?

Use the account deletion page. We'll email you a confirmation link.

Found a bug or security issue?

Email us with your device, app version and what happened. Security reports get priority.

Haven't got the app yet?

Terra is free on the App Store and Google Play.
