Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Here is exactly what we collect and why.

Your privacy is important to us. It is our policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you across our app and website.

We only ask for personal information when we truly need it to provide a service to you. We collect it by fair and lawful means, with your knowledge and consent.

What we collect

We collect only information you provide to us. From your signup process, we collect your email address, username, and password.

When you make posts, we collect image metadata provided by you, and the image itself. It is stored on Cloudflare's servers.

When you make comments, all we collect is the text you have provided. For liking, viewing, following, etc. we just add a record to our database.

Basically, we don't collect any information other than what you provide to us, and we try to keep it to a minimum.

How we collect it

We collect your information through the signup process, post creation, comment creation, and other actions you take in our app.

Why we collect it

We collect your information to provide you with the services you have requested from us.

Where we store it

We store your information in our database and on Cloudflare's servers.

We believe in keeping your information secure and private. With this being said, however, if we are ever contacted by law enforcement, we will have to provide them with the information they request.

Third-party links

Our website may link to external sites that are not operated by us. Please be aware that we have no control over the content and practices of these sites, and cannot accept responsibility or liability for their respective privacy policies.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your experience. By using our app you agree to our use of cookies and the Terms of Service.

Your choices

You can delete your account and its data at any time from the account deletion page. Questions? Email support@terra.photos.
