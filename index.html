Why Terra

Social media, minus the noise.

Every other feed is about people. Terra is about places. One simple rule keeps the whole app feeling like a deep breath.

Nature-only, by design

No people in photos—just landscapes, wildlife, skies, streets and light. Posts that break the rule get reported and reviewed.

Curated, not algorithmic

Top Picks are standout photos chosen by people, plus a daily Photo of the Day spotlight.

Calm, mindful scrolling

No influencer culture, no comparing yourself to anyone. Just the world, seen through other people's eyes.

FAQ

Good questions

Is Terra free?

Yes. Terra is free to download and use on iPhone and Android.

Why no people in photos?

Because the world deserves the spotlight. Without selfies and portraits, the feed stays calm and focused on places, light and atmosphere—and there's nothing to compare yourself to.

What can I post?

Landscapes, skies, wildlife, plants, weather, cityscapes, streets, architecture—anything from the world around you, as long as there are no people in it and you took it yourself. You can post up to 10 photos at a time.

What happens if someone breaks the rules?

Tap Report on any post or comment. Our team reviews every report and removes content that doesn't belong. Read more in our Terms and Child Safety Standards.

Do I need to be a good photographer?

Not at all. Most of Terra is shot on phones. If something made you stop and look, it belongs here.

How do I delete my account?

You can request deletion from the account deletion page. We'll email you a link to confirm.
